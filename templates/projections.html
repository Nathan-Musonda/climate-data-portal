Understanding the two scenarios

These are possible futures, not exact predictions. They help compare lower and higher climate-pressure pathways.

Moderate scenario (SSP2-4.5)
If emissions slow
Countries make progress on reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Climate change continues, but at a slower pace.
High emissions scenario (SSP5-8.5)
If emissions keep rising
Global emissions remain high, creating stronger warming and larger climate shifts by 2040.
Method note: Projections combine the observed 2015-2025 trend with scenario adjustments and uncertainty bands. They are exploratory planning estimates, not official forecasts.
