Climate Data Explorer

Choose a province, district, year range, and climate variable to explore Zambia's temperature, rainfall, ground water levels, and drought patterns — all in one place. Filter as broadly or as specifically as you need.

🔍 How to use this explorer
1
Choose a province and district — or leave both as "All" to see the national picture.
2
Set a start and end year to focus on a specific period — for example 2019 to 2022.
3
Choose a climate variable — rainfall, daytime heat, night-time temperature, or ground water level.
4
The chart, summary cards, and table all update automatically. Use Download to save the filtered data as a file.
