National summary
Between 2015 and 2025, Zambia experienced a consistent rise in average temperatures of 0.9°C alongside a reduction in annual rainfall of approximately 87 mm. The Southern and Western provinces recorded the most significant declines in rainfall, while the Copperbelt and Northern provinces experienced the highest frequency of flood events. These trends are consistent with regional projections for Southern Africa and have direct implications for rain-fed agriculture, hydropower generation, and water security across all 10 provinces.
