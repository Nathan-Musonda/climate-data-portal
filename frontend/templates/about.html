About The Portal

Zambia climate information, presented in a cleaner way for planning, research, and public understanding.

The portal brings rainfall, temperature, and drought conditions into one place so users can move from scattered climate readings to clear interpretation. It is designed to support evidence-based decisions without forcing people to work through many files or specialist tools.

Purpose

Why this platform exists and the problem it addresses.

Climate information is valuable, but it can be hard to understand when it is spread across many files, measures, and places. This platform brings Zambia-focused climate information into one easy-to-navigate space so patterns can be explored more quickly and more consistently.

It is built to support researchers, policymakers, practitioners, and the wider public with a practical view of rainfall variability, temperature patterns, and drought stress across Zambia's provinces and districts.

What the portal includes

The main indicator groups available across the platform.

Rainfall
  • Annual and monthly rainfall totals
  • Wet and dry year comparisons
  • Province-level rainfall differences
Temperature
  • Daytime and night-time temperature trends
  • District-level heat comparisons
  • Heatwave day frequency over time
Drought Conditions
  • Drought severity classification
  • Ground water level tracking
  • Province drought comparison
Exploration Tools
  • Dashboards and trend charts
  • Rankings and district comparisons
  • District-level map and data exploration

How to use it well

A simple interpretation flow for better reading of the data.

1
Set the scope.

Start with a province, district, or year range so the analysis answers a specific question.

2
Compare indicators together.

Read rainfall, temperature, and drought signals together instead of relying on one variable alone.

3
Use rankings and comparisons carefully.

Rankings and district comparisons are useful for identifying patterns, but they should still be interpreted with local knowledge and context in mind.

Methodology

How the portal summarizes data.

  • Summaries are produced from stored monthly and annual records.
  • Rankings and district comparisons use aggregated values to show differences across areas.
  • Trend views are designed to show variability over time, not just single values.

Who it serves

The audience the page is designed for.

  • Researchers studying rainfall, temperature, and drought variability.
  • Policymakers supporting planning and resilience decisions.
  • Farmers and practitioners in agriculture, water, and disaster preparedness.
  • Public users looking for understandable climate context.

Quick reading cues

Simple interpretation reminders.

Higher rainfall = wetter conditions Higher temperature = warmer conditions Lower ground water level may signal drought stress

Limitations

Important context for responsible use.

  • Coverage may vary by location and time period.
  • Aggregated views simplify local complexity.
  • The portal supports decisions, but it does not replace detailed technical assessment.
