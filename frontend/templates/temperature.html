Is temperature rising over time?
Shows whether daytime and night-time temperatures are increasing year by year.
What to look for: If both lines move upward over time, the area is getting consistently hotter. Even small increases can affect planting seasons, water demand, and heatwave risk.
How hot was each year?
Yearly average maximum and minimum temperature to identify the hottest and coolest years.
What to look for: Taller recent bars confirm warming. Unusually hot years may line up with regional heat and dry-season stress.
Which districts are hottest?
Compares the hottest districts in the selected year using average maximum temperature.
What this means: Districts at the top of this ranking carry stronger heat-stress risk for people, crops, livestock, and outdoor work.
Daily temperature range by month
Monthly maximum and minimum temperatures reveal seasonal heat and temperature range.
What this means: A wide gap means hot days and cooler nights. A narrow gap means nights stay warm, which can increase discomfort and crop stress.
How often does temperature reach each level?
Shows the spread of maximum temperature values and common heat ranges.
What this means: If the tallest bars are in higher temperature ranges, hot days are more common than cool days. This matters for heat stress and water planning.
What does rising temperature mean for Zambia?

Warmer temperatures can reduce soil moisture, shorten planting windows for rain-fed crops, increase heat stress for people and livestock, and raise water demand. Understanding where and when heat peaks helps communities plan farming, health responses, and water use more effectively.

About this data

This page summarizes temperature patterns across Zambia by district. It shows daytime heat and cooler night-time conditions over time.
