What this site collects

The portal has no user accounts, sign-up forms or comment features, and it does not ask you for personal details. Like most web servers, ours records basic request logs (time, requested page, status code) to keep the service running and diagnose faults.

Analytics

{% if ga_measurement_id %}This site uses Google Analytics with IP anonymisation to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies in your browser for this purpose.{% else %}This site does not currently use analytics cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated.{% endif %}

Third-party services

To display pages and maps, your browser loads files from content delivery networks and map services, including jsDelivr, Cloudflare (cdnjs), unpkg, and ArcGIS for district boundaries. These providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests, under their own privacy policies.

Data shown on the portal

The climate figures are environmental data about districts and provinces, not information about individuals.
