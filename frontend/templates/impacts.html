The bigger picture - what four variables tell us together

Looking at temperature, rainfall, floods, and drought together reveals Zambia's pattern of overlapping climate difficulties. Hotter conditions increase evaporation, low rainfall deepens drought pressure, and intense rain after dry periods can still trigger flooding because the ground cannot absorb water quickly enough. This page is designed to show both the local district story and the wider national pattern in one place.
